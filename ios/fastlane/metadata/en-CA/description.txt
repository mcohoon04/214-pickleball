214 Pickle is a pickleball court scheduling app for groups who share a court. Create your court, invite your players, and manage your schedule — all in one place.

CREATE YOUR COURT
Own or manage a pickleball court? Create your court in seconds, get a unique court code, and share it with your players to get started.

SHARED CALENDAR
See all bookings at a glance with a clean month view. Green dots show which days have games scheduled. Tap any day to see the full hour-by-hour schedule.

EASY BOOKING
Pick an available time slot (5 AM – 9 PM), choose how long (1-4 hours), select 2-4 players from your group, and book the court with one tap. Everyone gets notified instantly.

REAL-TIME UPDATES
Bookings appear live for all members — no refreshing needed. See what's available right now, without calling or texting.

NOTIFICATIONS
Get push notifications and emails when you're added to a game, when a booking is cancelled, and a reminder 1 hour before your match.

FLEXIBLE SCHEDULING
Book 1 to 4 hour sessions. Players can leave a booking or add others without needing the organizer. The court is yours to manage as a group.

COURT CODE ACCESS
Each court has a unique code. Share it with your players — only people with the code can see and book your court. Your group, your court, your schedule.

Built for any group who shares a pickleball court. No payments, no ads, no complexity. Just book and play.
